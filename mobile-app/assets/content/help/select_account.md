Start here. This screen lists every email account you have added, along with the last-scan status. Tap an account to open Manual Scan for that inbox. Use the "+" button to add a new account.

Each account has a star. Tap the star to make that account your default: the star fills and "Default account" appears under the address. Settings and scans then use that account without asking which one. Tap the star again to remove the default. You can have one default account at a time.

Use the trash can icon on an account to delete all of its data. Deleting the default account also removes the default.
