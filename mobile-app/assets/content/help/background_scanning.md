Background Scanning runs the same rules engine as Manual Scan, but on a schedule -- the app wakes up through the operating system's scheduler without an open window. Scan Mode, Scan Range, and Default Folders are shared with Manual Scan (see that section above).

Background Scanning is configured **per account**. The Background tab shows which account you are configuring (see the account header at the top of the tab), and each enabled account gets its own scheduled entry that runs on its own interval. Enabling it on one account does not enable it on the others.

The top of the Background tab reads, in order: the Enable switch, "Scan every", "Scan when new mail arrives" (Android), and the Android battery and timing notes. Then come Test, Scan Mode, Scan Range, Default Folders, and Debug.

- Enable: per-account on/off switch. Off removes that account's scheduled task; on registers a scheduled entry for that account with the OS scheduler. Disabled by default on fresh installs.
- Scan every: how often this account's scheduled scan runs. It appears only while background scanning is on for this account. Pick an interval from the list (5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes; 1, 2, 4, 12 or 24 hours), or pick Custom... to enter any other value: a number and Minutes or Hours. The shortest interval is 5 minutes, to limit battery use; the longest is 24 hours. A custom entry outside that range is flagged and cannot be saved. Each account has its own interval. A daily scan is 24 hours. Daily with Scan Range = 1 day is the most efficient continuous-monitoring setup. To limit simultaneous scans, an interval longer than 15 minutes starts up to 5 minutes earlier or later than the exact time; a 15 minute or shorter interval runs exactly on time.
- Scan when new mail arrives (Android): a per-account switch. When on, a new-mail notification from your mail app starts a scan of that account right away, in addition to the schedule. The Gmail app triggers Gmail accounts, the AOL app triggers AOL accounts, and Yahoo Mail triggers Yahoo accounts. Samsung Email and Outlook trigger every account that has this switch on. The account's own Enable switch must also be on. The app learns only which app posted the notification, never its title or text. Android asks you to allow Notification access the first time you turn it on.
- Android timing note: Android runs background scans when the phone allows. While the phone is idle, expect up to about 45 minutes between scans, even with a shorter interval. Opening the app runs any work that was waiting.
- Test: runs the background pipeline once, immediately, so you can verify the scheduler, credentials, and rules all line up before trusting the scheduled run. Useful after an upgrade or a config change.
- Debug (Export after each scan): typically off. When on, every background run adds its results to a CSV in the scan_exports folder inside the export folder (MyEmailSpamFilter in Documents unless you chose another). Useful when diagnosing a rule that seems to misfire or building an audit trail.
